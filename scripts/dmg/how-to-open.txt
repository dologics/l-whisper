INSTALL
1. Drag "L - Whisper" onto the Applications folder (in this window).
2. Open Applications and double-click L - Whisper.

FIRST OPEN (once)
L - Whisper isn't from the Mac App Store, so macOS blocks it the first time:
3. You'll see "L - Whisper Not Opened" (or "can't be opened"). Click Done / OK.
4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway"
   next to L - Whisper. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
5. Open L - Whisper again. A short setup guides you through permissions, your
   dictation key and downloading a speech model (one time, from the official
   whisper.cpp files).

Wrong DMG? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" = Apple Silicon DMG,
"Processor: Intel" = Intel DMG.

Needs macOS 14.2 (Sonoma) or newer. Your audio and text never leave your Mac.
