Welcome to L - Whisper

Speech to text that runs entirely on this Mac. A few quick steps and you're ready.

  • Dictation — hold a key, speak, and the text appears where your cursor is.
  • Transcribe files — drop an audio or video file, get a timestamped transcript.
  • Meetings — record your calls (your mic + the other side) and transcribe them.
  • Library — every meeting and transcribed file in one list, kept in Documents.
  • Private — your audio and text never leave this Mac.
